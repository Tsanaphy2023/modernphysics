# บลูพริ้นต์การผลิตวิดีโอโปรโมท: AI Coding & Python Workshop

## Phase 2: Global Definitions

### 1. Visual Style Specification
- **Sub-genre:** Modern 3D Cinematic / Tech Futuristic
- **Rendering + Line:** Photorealistic 3D rendering with clean geometric precision, smooth material surfaces (glass, brushed metal, glowing silicon circuits).
- **Color + Lighting:** Deep charcoal gray backgrounds (#121212), vibrant electric blue (#007BFF) and cyan glowing neon lighting, subtle rim lighting to emphasize 3D forms.
- **Detail density:** High detail on futuristic lab environments, glowing data streams, and floating code syntax.

### 2. Audio Profiles
- **Off-screen Narrator:** Male, deep and resonant tone, calm, formal, and trustworthy pace (Thai language).
- **BGM (Separate):** Electronic Corporate Tech, modern and inspiring, steady 120 BPM, featuring ambient synths, crisp electronic percussion, and inspiring melodic arpeggios.
