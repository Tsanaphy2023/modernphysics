# Phase 11 Completion Report: Chapter 8 Enhanced Implementation

## Overview
Phase 11 has been successfully completed with the creation of Chapter 8 Enhanced, focusing on "จริยธรรมและผลกระทบทางสังคมของ AI ในฟิสิกส์" (AI Ethics and Social Impact in Physics).
