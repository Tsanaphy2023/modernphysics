
## Smart Farming: How Automation Is Transforming Agriculture (Eastern Peak)

- **Smart farming (or farm automation):** Various tech innovations in traditional farming to optimize food production and improve quality.
- Addresses challenges like growing population, labor shortages, and consumer demand for eco-friendly sustenance.

### Ways to use automation in agriculture:

#### Robots and autonomous machines:
- Ideal for repetitive, labor-intensive tasks in the field (harvesting, watering, seeding).
- **Automated tractors:** Remote control possible, becoming more independent with vision systems, light detection tools, GPS.
    - *Example:* Bear Flag Robotics builds driverless tractors for remote control, route planning, and real-time reports.
- **Seeding and weeding robotics:** Work with great precision, use AI and computer vision to reduce pesticides and produce high-quality food.
    - *Example:* Eco Robotics produces autonomous, solar-powered robots for precise herbicide spraying.
- **Automatic irrigation:** Combines Subsurface Drip Irrigation (SDI) with IoT sensors to monitor moisture levels and send real-time analytics.
    - *Example:* The RAPID project offers cost-effective, robot-assisted precision irrigation.
- **Harvest automation:** Robots designed to gently harvest fruits and vegetables.
    - *Examples:* Abundant (apple harvesting using vacuum), Agrobot (strawberry harvesting, determines ripeness via AI).

#### Drones:
- Cheaper and easier alternative to helicopters/satellites for aerial monitoring.
- **Monitoring and analytics:** Identify damaged crops using different light types (including near-infrared), provide real-time information to farmers.
    - Useful for creating planting schemes and 3D field maps with soil analysis.
- **Planting:** Robots can seed from the air.
    - *Example:* DroneSeed uses drones to reforest lands after wildfires by shooting seeds with compressed air.
- **Irrigation:** Drones can water plants, adapting to location factors (altitude, objects, weather).
    - *Example:* DJI's Agras MG-1 drone can carry 10 liters of liquid and cover large areas quickly.
    - Agrobots can communicate to optimize watering based on plant needs.

### Benefits of farm automation:
- **Meets consumer demands:** Provides fresh, quickly delivered food, saving time and money for farmers.
- **Decreases labor shortage:** Reduces the need for human labor in monotonous tasks.
- **More eco-friendly agriculture:** Robots can apply pesticides with great precision, reducing environmental impact.
